# AURELIA

**AURELIA — Stochastic dynamics, golden ratios and early cognitive trajectories**

---

## Overview

**AURELIA** is a mathematical–physical–biological research pipeline designed to model, infer and predict the stochastic dynamics of complex biological systems, with a primary focus on early trajectories of cognitive decline.

The name *AURELIA* deliberately evokes the organic and vital: it carries the root of the golden section (*aurea sectio*) while resonating with living systems — growth, form, and the emergent patterns that biology repeatedly discovers in nature. Just as Fibonacci ratios and phyllotactic arrangements appear across scales of life, AURELIA seeks the same signatures of optimality and self-similarity inside high-dimensional biological and clinical data.

The project unifies discrete Markovian memory, continuous stochastic processes, Bayesian inference and the detection of golden-ratio structure into a coherent framework that can later absorb molecular biomarkers and modern neural architectures for translational impact.


*"O nome Aurelia remete à ideia de harmonia da natureza, inspirando-se no conceito de 'Áureo' — da Razão Áurea e do Número de Ouro, padrões matemáticos presentes na vida.

O 'L' representa Life (a vida em sua dimensão humana e biológica), enquanto o 'IA' destaca a Inteligência Artificial, responsável pela modelagem preditiva que investiga a trajetória do declínio cognitivo em doenças neurodegenerativas."*

## Scientific Pipeline

The core methodological sequence is:

1. **First-order Markov chains** — baseline stochastic memory and transition structure
2. **Higher-order Markov models** — longer temporal dependencies and richer memory kernels
3. **Expanded state-space representations** — embedding of latent or augmented variables that capture hidden biological degrees of freedom
4. **Continuous-time stochastic processes** — derivation and numerical treatment of the Fokker–Planck equation (and related forward/backward Kolmogorov equations) to describe the evolution of probability densities
5. **Bayesian inference** — rigorous parameter estimation, uncertainty quantification and model comparison
6. **Golden-ratio / Fibonacci signature detection** — identification of φ-related scaling, self-similarity or optimal ratios inside empirical samples and inferred trajectories
7. **Numerical prediction** — forward simulation and early-warning forecasts of system evolution

This cascade is intentionally modular: each stage can be validated independently and then composed into end-to-end predictive pipelines.

## Biological & Organic Motivation

Biological systems are inherently stochastic, multi-scale and history-dependent. Cognitive trajectories — particularly the slow, often silent progression toward mild cognitive impairment and dementia — exemplify this complexity. Molecular markers (proteomic, transcriptomic, metabolomic, imaging-derived) do not evolve in isolation; they diffuse, interact and drift inside high-dimensional state spaces whose geometry may itself harbour golden-ratio organisation.

AURELIA treats these processes as living dynamical systems:

- Transition probabilities and memory kernels mirror cellular and network-level decision processes.
- Fokker–Planck descriptions capture the diffusive spreading of molecular concentrations and the probabilistic evolution of cognitive states.
- Bayesian updating formalises the continual incorporation of new biomarker evidence.
- The appearance of Fibonacci / φ structure is interpreted as a possible signature of evolutionary or developmental optimisation — a bridge between pure mathematics and the organic logic of growth, packing and information transmission in living tissue.

By grounding abstract stochastic machinery in biologically meaningful state variables and clinically relevant endpoints, AURELIA aims to move from descriptive modelling toward actionable early detection.

---

## From Stochastic Foundations to Neural Architectures

The pipeline is designed to evolve naturally into hybrid neuro-symbolic or fully data-driven architectures:

- Markov and Fokker–Planck components can serve as structured inductive biases or physics-informed layers inside neural networks.
- Bayesian inference supplies uncertainty-aware training objectives and calibration.
- Golden-ratio features may act as interpretable latent coordinates or regularisers. 
- End-to-end models will integrate longitudinal molecular / imaging biomarkers with clinical cognitive scores to produce probabilistic forecasts of decline.

The long-term vision is a translational platform capable of supporting precision monitoring and timely intervention strategies.
